Sacral somatic dysfunctions — OMM Level 1

A schematic, rotatable 3D model of the sacrum with the 10 sacral somatic dysfunctions and their landmark, seated flexion, sphinx, spring, and L5 findings. Drag the model to orbit it freely — each preset rocks the sacrum about its axis toward the dysfunctional position. Torsions turn about the oblique axis; shears slide one side up or down.

Drag to orbit freely. Posterior view shown by default — left side of the model is the patient's left.

posterior (shallow sulcus / posterior ILA) anterior (deep sulcus / anterior ILA)

Pick a dysfunction above to see its exam findings. The model will oscillate to show the motion.

Findings from the Atlas of Osteopathic Techniques 4e, ch 10 (sacral MET diagnosis blocks), and Foundations of Osteopathic Medicine 5e, ch 38 (Table 38.1) and ch 31 (Table 31.4). Where the two texts differ, both are shown. The OMM written midterm case (positive left seated flexion test with a left-on-right torsion) agrees. ILA findings are relative to the other side. This is a schematic model, not a scanned anatomical mesh.
