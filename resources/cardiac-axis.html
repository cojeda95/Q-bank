These six leads are one axis. Move the arrow to your guess, then check.

  • Normal −30° to +90°
  • Left axis deviation −30° to −90°
  • Right axis deviation +90° to +180°

0° is lead I, to the right. Positive angles run clockwise. Net QRS height is cos(axis − lead angle): the same direction as a lead is positive, the opposite direction is negative, and a right angle to the lead is equiphasic.
